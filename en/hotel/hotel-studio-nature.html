Hotel Studio with Natural View

The studios are located on the park of the Resort, on the ground floor.

  • 2 boxspring beds with double top mattress (incl. bed linen), possibility for extra bed
  • Private terrace with garden furniture and natural view
  • Sitting area in the bedroom and small table with two chairs
  • Air conditioning
  • Bathroom with shower, toilet, hairdryer and towels
  • Flatscreen TV
  • Safe
  • Kitchenette with refrigerator, stove, kettle, coffee facilities and cleaning kit (no kitchen linen)
  • Pets allowed (on request)
