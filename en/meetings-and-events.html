NATURAL SERENITY

“Between the gentle sound of the surf and the tranquility of De Graevelinge.”

Exclusive Peace & Space

Room for Inspiration Between Land & Sea

Meet 100 metres from the beach, surrounded by dunes and serene Zeeland nature for maximum focus and fresh insights.

Resort Land & Zee offers a tranquil, inspiring and natural environment for business gatherings of all kinds: from strategic executive meetings, offsites and workshops to teambuilding days and multi-day corporate retreats. With bright, modern rooms accommodating up to 100 people and views over the restored De Graevelinge tidal arm, you combine focused working sessions with the invigorating energy of the Zeeland coastline.

Winter offsites in pure peace & focus

Especially during the quieter winter months, Scharendijke provides the ideal setting for an inspiring offsite or team retreat. Far away from daily distractions, you will find the silence and concentration required for genuine depth — refreshed by a brisk beach walk just 100 metres away.
